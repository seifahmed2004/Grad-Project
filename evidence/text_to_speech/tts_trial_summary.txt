Text-to-Speech Trial Summary

Owner:
Mohamed Osama Darwish

Old Trial:
The first functional TTS attempt used a Tacotron-style acoustic model with Griffin-Lim waveform reconstruction.

Result:
The model generated audio, but the sound was robotic and buzzy.

Reason:
The limitation was caused by Griffin-Lim, which is a classical vocoder and does not learn realistic waveform generation.

Improvement:
To improve audio quality while keeping the project fully from scratch, a HiFi-GAN neural vocoder was implemented from zero and added as the next stage of the TTS pipeline.
